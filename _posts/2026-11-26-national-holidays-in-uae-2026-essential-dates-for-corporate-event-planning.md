## Key National Holidays in UAE 2026

Here are the anticipated public holidays for 2026, ideal for planning standout corporate events or incentive travel in the UAE:

- **New Year’s Day** – January 1  
  The year begins with global celebrations, making it a popular choice for launching annual business strategies or hosting glamorous gala dinners.

- **Eid Al Fitr** – Expected to fall around March 20-23  
  Marking the end of Ramadan, Eid Al Fitr is a time for joyful gatherings, ideal for high-end event production and VIP event services. Note that dates are subject to the lunar calendar and may shift slightly.

- **Arafat Day and Eid Al Adha** – Expected June 28-30  
  These holidays commemorate the Hajj pilgrimage and are central to the UAE’s cultural calendar. They provide a unique opportunity for culturally immersive experiences and exclusive group event coordination.

- **Islamic New Year (Hijri New Year)** – Expected July 17  
  A moment for reflection and renewal, often marked by quieter business activity—an opportune time for executive retreats or team-building experiences in the UAE.

- **Prophet Muhammad’s Birthday** – Expected August 6  
  Another significant date, offering the chance to connect more deeply with Emirati heritage.

- **Commemoration Day** – December 1  
  Honoring the nation’s heroes, this day is typically observed with respect and remembrance.

- **UAE National Day** – December 2-3  
  The highlight of the year, marked by spectacular festivities, parades, and fireworks. This is the pinnacle for luxury event management and Dubai corporate hospitality.

*Please note: Islamic holidays are based on the Hijri calendar and subject to official moon sightings. Confirmed dates will be announced by UAE authorities closer to each occasion.*
