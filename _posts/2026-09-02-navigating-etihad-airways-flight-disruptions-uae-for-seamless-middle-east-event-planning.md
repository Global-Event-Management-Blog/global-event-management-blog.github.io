## Best Practices for Managing Flight Disruptions During Middle East Business Conferences and Incentive Travel

Drawing on decades of group event coordination, we recommend the following strategies:

- **Staggered arrivals**: For large groups, schedule arrivals over multiple flights to mitigate risk.
- **Pre-arranged transport**: Secure flexible, on-demand transfers that adapt to schedule changes.
- **Communication protocols**: Ensure all stakeholders—guests, venues, and suppliers—are updated instantly about travel changes.
- **Comprehensive travel insurance**: Protect your investment in MICE travel with robust coverage.
