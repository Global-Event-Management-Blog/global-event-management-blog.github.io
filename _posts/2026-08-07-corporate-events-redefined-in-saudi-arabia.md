- **State-of-the-art infrastructure**: Cutting-edge convention centers, luxury hotels, and unique event venues ready to host gatherings of any size.

- **Cultural authenticity**: Opportunities abound for immersive experiences—think desert safaris, traditional music, and culinary journeys that provide a genuine connection to local heritage.
